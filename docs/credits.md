# Project and attribution

Enzyme Cascade Design was developed within [TACsy](https://tacsy.eu/), the
Training Alliance for Computational Systems Chemistry. TACsy connects computer
science and systems chemistry to develop computational methods for studying
chemical reaction networks. Enzymatic synthesis design is one of its application
areas. The example networks and kinetic models retain their source annotations
and reaction identities.

TACsy is supported by the European Union's Marie-Skłodowska-Curie Actions,
[grant agreement 101072930](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101072930).

## Software dependencies

MØD supplies graph rewriting and network expansion. Catalyst and
DifferentialEquations.jl supply the reaction-system and ODE machinery. These
dependencies are installed separately and retain their own licences.

## Supplied pathway results

The saved pathway records use the method described in Banke et al.,
*Carbon Support in Metabolic Pathway Realization Landscapes*, manuscript in
preparation (2026). This repository supplies those example results and their viewer.
